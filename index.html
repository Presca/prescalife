I observe. I ponder. I create.

Attention is my first design tool. I watch how people pause, where they smile, what they almost say — the quiet details most products walk past. Then I sit with them, turn them over, and shape them into something people can hold. ✦

The river so far

Careers aren't ladders. Mine flows more like water — finding its own way, picking up light as it goes.

  1. the source

    UX Designer

    Years of listening — research, flows, wireframes, usability. Learning that good design starts with paying attention, not with pixels.

  2. gathering speed

    Product Designer

    Zooming out from screens to systems. Owning problems end-to-end — strategy, story, and craft — and caring about what ships, not just what's mocked.

  3. a new current

    Building with AI

    AI turned my sketches into software. I design and build now — pairing with AI tools to explore wider, prototype in hours, and ship what I imagine.

  4. open water

    Solopreneur

    My own path, my own products. Small, thoughtful things made with childlike wonder and grown-up rigor — released into the world under my own name.
